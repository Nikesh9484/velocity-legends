Artwork

Track loading art, grandstand crowd, pit building, sponsor boards, building fronts, horizon panoramas and tree sprites were generated for this game with Picsart (Flux 2 Pro). All car names and sponsor brands are fictional.

Sound

Engine sound loops were generated for this game with ElevenLabs sound effects (via Picsart).
